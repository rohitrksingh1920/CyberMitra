Information about official contact channels and where to report urgent cyber-fraud incidents in India.

How to reach CyberMitra

CyberMitra is a cyber-safety awareness and educational initiative. We do not currently publish a public support email address, Discord community, GitHub issue tracker, or social-media contact account on this website.

We have intentionally not listed unverified contact details. This page will be updated if an official contact channel is established.

Reporting an online financial cyber fraud in India?

For an urgent cyber-fraud report, use the Government of India’s official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or call the cyber-fraud helpline.

Cyber-fraud helpline: 1930

Open the official cybercrime portal ↗

CyberMitra is not a government website, police service, emergency response service, or substitute for filing a report with the relevant authorities.

Before contacting anyone

  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, card PINs, or full payment credentials with anyone claiming to represent CyberMitra.
  • Use only official government websites and verified channels when reporting a crime.
  • Do not treat information on this awareness website as legal advice.
